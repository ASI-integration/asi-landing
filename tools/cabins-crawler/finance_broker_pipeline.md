## ASI product-specific finance thesis

ASI Cabins installs repeatable, fully enclosed **two-person private modular cabins inside leased commercial premises**. The financial question is the incremental cash-generating capacity of a legally convertible leased property, **not** construction or purchase of a conventional hotel or hotel rooms.

Potential finance stack: (1) landlord contribution / fitting-out allowance / rent-free period; (2) equipment or asset finance on cabins and qualified durable equipment, where lender accepts the asset and its recoverability; (3) renovation / fit-out lending on lawful conversion; (4) local sponsor / operator equity or revenue share; (5) where eligible, government-backed tourism or automation funding. Never assume cabling, plumbing, cabins attached to the building, or leasehold improvements automatically qualify as removable, lender-financeable equipment. Local fire, building, accommodation licence, lease, insurance, taxes, egress and accessibility law still apply.

## Qualification gates before any broker mandate

1. **Deal size**: what total investment range, minimum cheque size, existing-business track record and loan-to-value or debt coverage required?
2. **Asset and jurisdiction**: can financing be secured on removable cabins/equipment within **leased** premises; is the landowner/leaseholder local partner mandatory; who owns the cabins if the lease ends?
3. **Operator and local permissions**: legal hotel/accommodation classification, zoning, fire egress, ventilation, wet areas, accessibility, short stays, guest registration and insurance.
4. **Capital type**: loans, equipment leasing, equity, landlord capex participation, revenue share, or blended? Repayment, covenants, foreign ownership and FX restrictions.
5. **Fees and incentives**: fee payable only on successful funding where commercially reasonable; disclose retainer, broker/referral fees, exclusivity, co-broking splits and conflicts; verify licence.
6. **Investor evidence**: anonymous indications of comparable deals, underwriting process, funding timeline, conditions precedent; no vague 'guaranteed funding'.
