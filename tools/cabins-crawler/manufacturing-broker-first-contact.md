## Initial enquiry (English)

**Subject:** Supplier sourcing and engineering partner inquiry — modular indoor cabin system (ASI)

Hello,

We are developing **ASI Cabins**, a standardized modular **indoor private accommodation system** to be installed inside existing leased commercial buildings. We are looking for an independent China-side **manufacturing sourcing and engineering partner**, rather than a seller of ready-made outdoor Apple Cabins.

The reference module is approximately **5.8 × 2.2 m / 12.8 m²** for **two adults**, with an enclosed privacy-first shell, proper acoustic isolation (including operating ventilation), full private shower/WC, modular assembly, electrical/plumbing interfaces, access control and serviceable components. The design needs to be manufacturable as transportable and/or panelized **kits assembled inside existing buildings**, with local approvals considered from the beginning.

We would like to assess a **first prototype / 3–5 unit pilot**, with optional later production of **20, 50 and 100 units**, subject to technical validation, a suitable site and funding.

Could you confirm:

1. Whether you have experience sourcing or engineering **full-size modular rooms, sanitary pods, acoustic enclosures or complex building-interior kits**, rather than small consumer products.
2. Whether you can independently identify and assess **three or more named OEM factories/integrators** and show us the factory price separately from your fees.
3. Your suggested scope for an initial feasibility/sourcing study, deliverables, fee structure and timeline.
4. Whether you support factory audits, DFM, prototyping, tested assembly acoustics/fire materials, quality inspection, and export documentation.
5. How you manage conflicts of interest, supplier commissions, drawing/IP ownership and confidentiality agreements.

At this stage we are seeking a **nonbinding capability discussion only**, not placing an order. If there is a fit, we can provide our engineering brief under an appropriate confidentiality agreement.

Best regards,  
ASI Global — ASI Cabins project
