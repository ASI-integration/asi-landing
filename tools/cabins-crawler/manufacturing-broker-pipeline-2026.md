## Required broker mandate (stage-gated, NOT signed)

1. **No vendor lock-in:** shortlist ≥3 **named actual OEM factories/integrators** with address, legal registration, audited capabilities, engineering staff, recent production references. Separate panel, acoustics, wet room, HVAC and tech subsuppliers as needed. Where necessary, propose one integrator responsible for quality/warranty.
2. **Engineering feasibility** against the owner-approved brief: actual interior footprint, panelization/doorway transport plan, finished bathroom waterproofing, waste/sewer/electric connections, room ventilation and acoustic cross-talk, safe fire-rated materials and evacuation clearances, universal access and local inspector sign-offs. No invented certification.
3. **Acoustic acceptance:** proposed Silent Shell wall/door assembly targets and test methods stated in RFQ, e.g. laboratory STC for assemblies and *post-install, HVAC-on* sound isolation. Do not claim a number is achieved without a test; noise leakage around shafts and doors matters.
4. **Comparable quotes** for **1 engineered prototype**, then conditional **3–5**, **20**, **50**, **100** units: same BOM, options, packaging, site installation and commissioning, spare parts, warranty; separate engineering/NRE, tooling, factory EXW/FOB, freight, taxes, site MEP and compliance. Price by *installable cabin system*, not supplier stock "Apple Cabin".
5. **Broker economics:** flat sourcing scope/fee, retainer, success fee or order-margin; conflict-of-interest and supplier kickback disclosures; inspection milestone schedule; no automatic exclusivity or large prepayment; milestone delivery only for verified output.
6. **IP & ownership:** two-stage release. Phase 1 nonconfidential capability teaser, Phase 2 NDA/China-ready NNN reviewed by legal counsel for detailed drawings, software protocols, BOM and acoustic design; retain access to toolings, test records, shop drawings, serials and spares. Do not prematurely disclose ASI source code.
7. **First pilot location:** no shipping container assumption until the target country/building has site-entry measurements and approved use. Packaging/assembly methodology is part of commercial competitiveness.
8. **Payment:** initial qualification is research only; owner explicitly approves a named contractor and fixed scope/fee before spending or outreach. No factory finance/grants assumed.

## One-stage decision before finance brokers

**Goal of first broker response:** written yes/no on ability to run a buyer-side manufacturing study for an **indoor, privacy-first, two-person acoustic modular unit with bathroom**, indicative study scope/fee and timeline, whether they can introduce **3 named certified/verified factories**, whether they have **large assembled module/panel kit** experience, and whether they accept the **3–5 prototype pilot + future 20/50/100** production economics.

Only after that: paid engineering discovery (if owner chooses), comparative supplier quotes, prototype design and tested landed cost. THEN finance broker deck can contain a credible capex and manufacturing contract. Financial brokers must not be asked to finance an imaginary BOM.
