# Residential decision layer — engineering handoff

**Scope:** Pure interpretation layer on top of `LocationAnalysis` (commercial engine + environment). Passes **1–4** are closed at **baseline-v4-pass4-edge-hardening** (2026-04-19): **30/30** control gates, **0** known gaps in the synthetic suite.

**Primary implementation:** `src/lib/location/residential-analysis.ts` — `buildResidentialAnalysis`.

---

## 1. What this layer does

From a full `LocationAnalysis`, it derives **five** product-facing outputs (no commercial score mutation):

| Output | Role |
|--------|------|
| `residentialAudienceType` | Livability-first audience bucket (`premium_comfort`, `mixed_use_adjacent`, `standard_residential`) |
| `residentialStrategy` | STR vs hybrid vs mid-term vs selective premium vs cautious |
| `operationalSuitability` | `full_auto` / `semi_auto` / `manual` for ops automation safety |
| `confidence` + `confidenceReasons` | Signal clarity and risk honesty |
| `strategyRationaleRu`, `operationalNoteRu`, `premiumComfortSignals` | Copy and explainability |

**Wiring:** `buildAnalysis` in `src/lib/location/gravity-scoring.ts` attaches `residentialAnalysis: buildResidentialAnalysis(baseAnalysis)`.

## 3. High-level decision flow

Order inside `buildResidentialAnalysis`:

1. **Audience** (`computeResidentialAudienceType`) — gates `premium_comfort` away from elevated/high env, fallback, and weak stability/score.
2. **Strategy** (`computeResidentialStrategy`) — priority: **cautious** (double burdens, harsh stacks, weak score + elevated, etc.) → **selective_premium** (premium audience + friction/stability gates) → **short_term** (classic floors and/or **elevated-only** urban-core override and/or **seasonal lift** band) → **hybrid** → **mid_term**.
3. **Confidence** (`computeResidentialConfidence`) — clarity, burden stacking, cross-score tension, caps for hybrid + elevated and industrial/harsh-stack ceilings.
4. **Operational suitability** (`computeOperationalSuitability`) — maps strategy + confidence + env + stability (e.g. resort-like `short_term` with low stability → `semi_auto`).
5. **Rationale** (`buildStrategyRationaleRu`, `buildOperationalNoteRu`) — Russian one-liners tied to strategy, blockers, and confidence tier.

Details and family table: [`docs/residential-regression-gates.md`](./residential-regression-gates.md) §0.

## 8. Decision model overview (maintainable)

**Strategy families** (see regression gates §0): `cautious_manual_only`, `selective_premium_short_term`, `short_term` (classic, elevated-core override, seasonal lift), `hybrid`, `mid_term`.

**Where caution dominates:** double environment burdens (e.g. nightlife + industrial or nightlife + major roads), airport-like / harsh stacks aligned with control IDs (R03, R06, R12, R23, R28), weak location under elevated concern, `cautious_manual_only` always paired with **manual** ops and **low** confidence cap discipline.

**Where overrides are allowed:** only **elevated** (not `high`) for urban-core STR, with joint score/demand/seasonality/audience thresholds and industrial/nightlife/road/transit guards. Seasonal lift only in **68–71** demand band, **≥90** seasonality, low friction, non-elevated, plus **pass-4** location/evergreen floors.

**Confidence:** `high` only when signals are clean and caps allow; **hybrid + elevated** without prime-core exception stays **≤ medium**; industrial / harsh-stack ceilings per pass-3. `confidenceReasons` lists drivers.

**Rationale:** `buildStrategyRationaleRu` composes audience label, demand/seasonality/env, optional aggressive blocker line, and a confidence phrase — not hand-maintained per postcode.

## 9. Do not regress (checklist)

- [ ] Strong **core urban** cases (e.g. R25 pattern) must **not** be blanket-blocked from `short_term` solely because env is **elevated** when the guarded composite passes.
- [ ] **Fragile suburb / burden-heavy** archetypes (R08, R03, R06, R12, R23, R28) stay **cautious** or **mid_term** / **manual** as defined — no accidental `full_auto` or `short_term` on true double-burden rows.
- [ ] **Confidence** must not overstate contested **hybrid + elevated** or industrial-heavy hybrids (R10, R15, R17 intentions — **medium**, not spurious **high**).
- [ ] **Threshold boundaries:** classic STR **`strDemandMin` = 72** (inclusive); seasonal lift band **68–71**; **`seasonalLiftLocationMin` / `seasonalLiftEvergreenMin`** must remain meaningful (R29, R30).

Any threshold change: run full runner + pass-4 vitest; treat gate doc §9 matrix as authoritative.

## 10. Known limitations / future improvements

- **Confidence semantics:** `low` on all `cautious_manual_only` cases is deliberate but can read like “bad data” rather than “high certainty of risk” — possible future split (e.g. risk vs data dimensions); see `residential-validation-baseline.md` §3.
- **Copy:** `premium_comfort` with non-selective strategy (R05/R21 tension) may still want product copy outside this layer.
- **Model class:** Heuristic, OSM- and score-driven — strong for defined archetypes, not omniscient for every real address; live fetch variance is **out of scope** of the synthetic runner.
