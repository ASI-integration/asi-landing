==================================================

J. DISTANCE RULE FOR RESIDENTIAL

==================================================



Core residential radius:

\- Primary reporting radius for magnets: 1.0 km



Main rule:

\- For residential property analysis, user-facing magnets must primarily come from within 1.0 km of the property.

\- If a prime anchor is inside this radius, it should be considered eligible for surfacing, subject to allowlist + ranking + de-duplication rules.



Soft extension:

\- A small extension beyond 1.0 km is allowed only for truly exceptional, city-forming anchors.

\- Recommended soft maximum: 1.5 km

\- Do NOT routinely surface magnets beyond 1.0 km

\- Do NOT use long-distance weak relevance inflation



Examples of anchors that may justify soft extension up to \~1.5 km:

\- major railway station

\- airport only if materially relevant to the property context

\- major medical cluster

\- major university campus

\- central business district

\- major port / terminal

\- major transport interchange



Anything beyond the soft extension should normally NOT appear in the residential user-facing magnet report.



Reporting behavior:

\- Show all materially relevant prime anchors within the core 1.0 km radius, but still obey surfacing limits:

&#x20; - default: top 3

&#x20; - hard max: 5

\- If more than 5 eligible anchors exist within 1.0 km, surface only the strongest and most interpretable ones.

\- Never fill the report with weak or repetitive anchors just because they are inside the radius.



Distance wording:

\- If inside 1.0 km, prefer meters where possible

\- Slightly above 1.0 km, use km with one decimal if needed



Examples:

\- "Железнодорожный вокзал находится примерно в 650 м"

\- "Крупный медицинский центр расположен примерно в 900 м"

\- "Деловой кластер находится примерно в 1.2 км"





==================================================

K. PERSISTENT DEMAND RULE — 24/7/365

==================================================



Core rule:

For residential reporting, a magnet should be surfaced only if it acts as a stable, persistent people-attracting anchor.



Meaning:

\- It should attract people regularly and structurally

\- It should not depend only on rare events, occasional spikes, or weak local activity

\- It should behave as a real demand generator across the year, not as episodic noise



Required interpretation:

A surfaced residential magnet should be something that matters in a durable way:

\- daily

\- weekly

\- year-round

\- structurally visible in the city fabric



This is what "24/7/365" means in this system:

\- not literally infinite crowds every hour

\- but a stable, recurring, all-season urban demand anchor

\- not a one-off or occasional point of interest



Allowed examples of persistent anchors:

\- railway station

\- metro interchange

\- airport

\- major medical center

\- major hospital cluster

\- university / campus

\- CBD / office cluster

\- government cluster

\- major logistics hub

\- port / terminal

\- industrial zone with stable employment gravity

\- major prison / military object if used as friction context

\- major historic landmark with year-round visitation

\- major city-forming transport interchange



Conditional examples:

These should be shown only if they clearly behave as persistent demand anchors in that city, not just occasional destinations:

\- stadium

\- arena

\- exhibition center

\- congress center

\- expo center

\- major concert venue

\- resort waterfront

\- ski resort

\- beach resort cluster



If such anchors are mostly episodic / seasonal / event-driven rather than structurally persistent, they should NOT be surfaced in the residential magnet report.



Do NOT surface:

\- places that matter only sometimes

\- places that matter only for short bursts

\- places that are famous but not actually demand-forming for everyday residential accommodation logic

\- weak lifestyle clutter

\- Surface only anchors that are structurally persistent ("24/7/365" in the sense of durable recurring demand or durable friction)

\- Event-only / episodic / seasonal-only anchors should not be shown unless they clearly function as persistent city magnets

\- Allowlist membership alone is not enough: distance + persistence filters must also pass
